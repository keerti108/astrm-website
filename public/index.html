astrm licenses patented midsole geometry into the shoes brands already make.

The brand keeps its name, its silhouette and its customer. We change what's inside the shoe — a structural chassis that lets the foot splay, flex and spring back, inside a conventional sole unit.

The premise

Your foot was built to splay, flex and spring back. Shoes stopped it.

Barefoot shoes give the foot its motion back, but only by dictating the shape of the shoe. Conventional midsoles keep the silhouette and flatten the motion. Custom orthotics sit on top of the problem, one prescription at a time.

astrm is a third option: the motion, engineered into the sole unit itself, invisible in a mirror and legible on a pressure map.

What the chassis does

Three movements

Fig. 01 — plan

Toe splay

The forefoot spreads under load. The chassis gives it room to, rather than holding it in a taper.

Fig. 02 — section

Controlled pronation

The medial arch is guided through its natural roll during propulsion — not blocked, not left unsupported.

Fig. 03 — section

Elastic recoil

The arch stores load and returns it. The chassis is tuned to spring rather than to absorb and stay flat.
